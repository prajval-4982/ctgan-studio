Upload your real-world tabular dataset, train a Conditional GAN, and generate high-fidelity synthetic data — complete with statistical and ML evaluation.

🚀 Get Started 📊 How It Works
📂

Upload Dataset

Upload any CSV dataset — the system validates and previews it instantly.

🧹

Auto Preprocessing

Missing values are imputed and categorical columns are encoded automatically.

🧠

CTGAN Training

A Conditional Tabular GAN learns the joint distribution of your real data.

🔄

Generate Records

Create any number of synthetic rows that mirror the statistical properties of the original.

📈

Statistical Evaluation

Distribution histograms, correlation heatmaps, mean/std comparisons.

🤖

ML Utility Testing

Logistic Regression & Decision Tree trained on real vs. synthetic data.
